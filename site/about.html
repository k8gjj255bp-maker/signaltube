About & methodology

SignalTube checks the public RSS feed of every tracked channel daily and gives each new video a substance score from 0 to 100. Every score is explainable: open "Why this score" on any video.

Starting point: 50. Penalties: words in ALL CAPS (−6 each), an all-caps title (−10), hype phrases such as "shocking", "insane", "changes everything", "is dead" (−8 each), exclamation marks (−4 each, −6 more for "!!"), emojis in the title (−5 each), short question-bait titles (−4), a thin description (−8), link spam (−6). Bonuses: substance words such as paper, tutorial, lecture, deep dive, from scratch (+4 each, up to 4), a detailed description (up to +12), references such as arXiv or GitHub (+6), chapter timestamps (+4), and the channel's editorial tier (tier 1 deep/technical +20, tier 2 +8, tier 3 popular/hype-prone −8).

Summaries come from the creator's own description, with promo lines removed. Next: a transcript-based LLM scorer, which will be blended with this heuristic.

Views and star ratings come from the feed's media:statistics/starRating and are shown for context only. They don't change the score.
